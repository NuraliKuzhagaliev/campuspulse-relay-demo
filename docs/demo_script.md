RELAY preserves the evidence, calculates what fits in actual availability, asks for approval of exact calendar actions, and verifies what happened.” Show the conflict and page evidence, choose the deadline, repair, remove Wednesday to expose the shortage, then return to baseline and show a clearly labeled local receipt.
